## Future Expansion

The following areas are planned as later extensions of the NordicFlow simulation and have not yet been implemented:

- ERP business-process and root-cause analysis
- MRP and inventory-planning simulation
- procurement and supplier-improvement analysis
- ERP implementation, migration and UAT simulation
- AI-assisted ERP analytics
- executive ERP transformation case

These future stages will be developed incrementally while the completed Phase I portfolio remains available for review.
